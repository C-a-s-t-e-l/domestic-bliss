End of Tenancy

Secure Your Deposit Return

Moving house is stressful enough without worrying about whether the property will pass inspection. Our end of tenancy cleaning service is designed for tenants who want their full deposit returned, estate agents who need a property presentation-ready, and landlords who want their rental back on the market fast. We operate throughout County Durham, covering Durham City, Darlington, Bishop Auckland, Newton Aycliffe, Spennymoor, Chester-le-Street, and surrounding areas.

Comprehensive Move-Out Cleaning

We work to a detailed industry-standard checklist referenced by major letting agents and property managers across County Durham. This is not a surface-level clean — we work systematically through every room, removing limescale from bathroom fittings, degreasing oven interiors, cleaning the insides of kitchen cupboards, and ensuring the property is left in the condition your tenancy agreement requires. Optional carpet cleaning and upholstery treatment are also available as an add-on for properties that need it.

What’s Included in the Deep Clean?

  • Kitchen Deep Clean: Ovens, hobs, extractors, microwaves, fridge interiors, and the inside of all cupboards and drawers.
  • Bathroom Descaling: Removal of limescale and water stains from tiles, shower screens, baths, taps, and toilet bowls.
  • Interior Windows: All glass, window sills, and frames cleaned to a streak-free finish throughout the property.
  • Skirting Boards & Woodwork: All woodwork, doors, architraves, and light switches dusted and wiped down.
  • Floors & Carpets: Vacuuming and hard floor mopping throughout; professional carpet cleaning available on request.
  • Walls & Marks: Spot-cleaning of scuffs and fingerprints from painted surfaces where possible.

Fast Turnaround for Landlords

Empty properties mean lost rental income. Domestic Bliss offers rapid-response end of tenancy cleaning for property managers and landlords across Durham, Darlington, and Bishop Auckland. We aim to complete most cleans within 24–48 hours of your enquiry, minimising void periods and getting your property back on the letting market as quickly as possible.

We provide a full post-clean report on request, along with before-and-after photographs to support any deposit dispute resolution through your deposit protection scheme.
