Office Cleaning

Create a Productive Workspace

A clean office is not just about appearance — it directly affects the health, wellbeing, and productivity of your team. Research consistently shows that employees working in clean, well-maintained environments are more focused and take fewer sick days. Domestic Bliss provides professional office cleaning solutions across County Durham for businesses of all sizes, from small independent offices in Durham City to multi-floor commercial premises in Darlington and Newton Aycliffe.

Our Office Cleaning Process

We follow a meticulous room-by-room checklist to ensure no surface is overlooked and no area is left unsanitised. Before we start any new contract, we carry out a full walkthrough of your office to understand your layout, any sensitive areas, and your preferred cleaning schedule. Our staff are fully vetted, trained, and experienced in handling office environments — including working around expensive electronics and confidential documents.

Key Focus Areas:

  1. Workstation Sanitisation: Cleaning keyboards, monitors, telephone handsets, and desk surfaces — the areas where bacteria transfer most readily between employees.
  2. Communal Areas: Deep cleaning of breakrooms, shared kitchens, coffee stations, and communal seating areas to a food-safe standard.
  3. Washroom Hygiene: Full sanitisation of all restroom facilities, restocking of consumables, and a mid-day refresh service where required.
  4. Waste Management: Daily emptying of waste bins and recycling points to maintain a fresh, odour-free working environment.
  5. Reception & Entrance Areas: High-visibility areas cleaned and polished to create the best first impression for visiting clients and guests.
  6. Hard Floors & Carpets: Regular vacuuming, mopping, and spot-treatment to keep your office flooring looking professional day to day.
