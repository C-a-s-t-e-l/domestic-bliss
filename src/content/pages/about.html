Reliable Service is the Key to Our Success

Domestic Bliss was founded on the principle that a clean environment shouldn't be a luxury — it should be a standard. Based in Durham City, we serve landlords, property managers, homeowners, and business owners across County Durham with a level of attention to detail that reflects our genuine pride in the work we do. From regular domestic cleans in Darlington and Newton Aycliffe to multi-site commercial contracts in Bishop Auckland and Chester-le-Street, every job receives the same dedicated, professional approach.

Our team of locally based cleaners bring skill, reliability, and discretion to every property they work in. We invest in ongoing training, professional-grade equipment, and eco-friendly products because we believe doing the job properly is always worth it — and our clients across County Durham agree.

  • No hidden fees or unexpected charges.
  • Personalised cleaning checklists for every client.
  • Consistency guaranteed on every visit.
  • Fully insured and DBS checked staff.
  • Eco-friendly cleaning products available on request.
