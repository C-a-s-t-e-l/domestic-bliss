Commercial Cleaning

Reliable Commercial Cleaning in County Durham

Maintaining a clean and professional commercial environment is essential for the safety of your staff and the reputation of your business. Domestic Bliss specialises in providing comprehensive commercial cleaning contracts tailored to businesses across County Durham — from small retail units in Durham City and Newton Aycliffe to industrial warehouses in Bishop Auckland and Spennymoor.

Whether you manage a single office suite or a large multi-site facility, our experienced team delivers consistent, high-standard results that keep your premises compliant, presentable, and safe for everyone who walks through the door.

Expert Solutions for Large-Scale Facilities

Commercial properties face levels of foot traffic and daily use that domestic cleaning simply cannot address. Our commercial cleaning team is trained to handle large-scale environments using professional-grade equipment and eco-friendly cleaning agents. We understand the specific requirements of different commercial settings — from the hygiene standards demanded in food-preparation environments to the presentational standards required in customer-facing retail.

What Our Commercial Service Includes:

  • Daily & Weekly Maintenance: Consistent cleaning schedules to keep your premises pristine and fully operational.
  • Floor Care: Professional vacuuming, mopping, buffing, and hard floor maintenance tailored to your flooring type.
  • Sanitization: Thorough deep cleaning of high-touch areas — door handles, light switches, and shared surfaces — to reduce illness and maintain hygiene standards.
  • Window & Exterior Cleaning: Maintaining the professional first impression of your storefront, entrance, or facility frontage.
  • Waste Management: Regular collection and disposal of general waste and recycling to keep your site clean and compliant.
  • Communal Areas: Breakrooms, reception areas, stairwells, and corridors cleaned and restocked as needed.

Customised Cleaning Contracts
