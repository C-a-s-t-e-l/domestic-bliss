Regular Domestic

Your Home, Professionally Cleaned

There is nothing quite like coming home to a clean, fresh house. At Domestic Bliss, we provide reliable, regular domestic cleaning services for homes across County Durham — from terraced houses in Durham City to family homes in Darlington, Newton Aycliffe, Bishop Auckland, and beyond. We take care of the cleaning so you can spend your time on the things that matter.

Reliable and Vetted Cleaners

Inviting someone into your home requires trust. Every member of our domestic cleaning team is hand-picked, thoroughly interviewed, reference-checked, and trained to follow the Domestic Bliss Standard — a detailed cleaning method developed to ensure a consistent, thorough result on every visit. Wherever possible, we pair you with the same cleaner each time, so you can build real confidence in who is coming into your home.

Standard Weekly Tasks:

  • Kitchens: Polishing worktops and surfaces, cleaning hob rings, wiping down appliance exteriors, and clearing the sink area.
  • Living Rooms: Detailed dusting of all surfaces and shelves, vacuuming carpets and soft furnishings, and polishing hard floors.
  • Bathrooms: Full sanitisation of showers, baths, toilets, basins, taps, and mirrors to a hygienic finish.
  • Bedrooms: Dusting all furniture, vacuuming floors, and — on request — changing bed linens.
  • Hallways & Stairs: Vacuuming or sweeping all floor areas, dusting banisters, and wiping down skirting boards.

Flexible Schedules to Suit Your Life

Whether you need a weekly visit to maintain an already-clean home or a fortnightly deep clean to stay on top of things, we offer flexible scheduling with no long-term tie-ins required. Need to skip a week for a holiday or reschedule at short notice? No problem. Our goal is to provide a seamless service that fits your lifestyle rather than disrupting it.

We also carry out one-off domestic cleans for spring cleaning, pre-move, post-move, or ahead of a special occasion. Just tell us what you need and we’ll provide a transparent, fixed-price quote with no obligation.
