Regular Domestic

Your Home, Professionally Cleaned

There is nothing like the feeling of walking into a clean home after a long day. At Domestic Bliss, we provide high-quality, reliable home cleaning services that allow you to reclaim your weekends and enjoy your living space.

Reliable and Vetted Cleaners

Inviting someone into your home requires trust. That’s why every member of our domestic team is hand-picked, thoroughly vetted, and trained to follow the "Domestic Bliss Standard" of cleaning.

Standard Weekly Tasks:

  • Kitchens: Polishing surfaces, cleaning hobs, and wiping down appliances.
  • Living Rooms: Detailed dusting, vacuuming, and fluffing of cushions.
  • Bathrooms: Sanitizing showers, baths, toilets, and mirrors.
  • Bedrooms: Dusting all furniture and, upon request, changing bed linens.

Flexible Schedules to Suit Your Life

Whether you need a weekly visit to keep on top of the mess or a fortnightly deep clean, we offer flexible scheduling with no long-term tie-ins. Our goal is to provide a consistent service that fits seamlessly into your lifestyle.
